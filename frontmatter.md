# The Essays
*On startups, ambition, and the future*

Sam Altman

Since 2013, Sam Altman has written about startups, ambition, and the arrival of AI. His best essays are as useful today as when he published them, but they are scattered among blog posts on other topics. This book collects them in one place, newest first.
